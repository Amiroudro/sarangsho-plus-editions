THE EDITORIAL PROMISE

Clear facts. Honest context. Useful learning.

Every important story will pass through a consistent process. AI can help organise and explain information; it cannot turn an unverified claim into a fact.

01

Collect responsibly

Use accessible publisher feeds and permitted public sources. Keep original links and publication times.

02

Check the important details

Verify names, dates, places, figures and official decisions. Separate reported facts, claims and analysis.

03

Explain in both languages

Write natural Bangla and English versions with the same facts, quantities and uncertainty.

04

Turn knowledge into practice

Add exam relevance, vocabulary and questions only when they genuinely follow from the source material.

✓

No invented headlines. No hidden uncertainty.

This is a foundation preview. Live newspaper collection, article summaries and exam materials are not connected yet. The page will only label a story as current when its source and publication time have been collected and checked.
